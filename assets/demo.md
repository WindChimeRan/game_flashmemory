# OOM — Stage 0 demo reel

This file exists to exercise the layout engine: streaming append, live
resize reflow, and the expand/collapse animation that becomes the game's
signature move.

The archivist had one rule: never keep more than the room could hold. Every
evening she walked the shelves with a lantern and decided what the library
would remember tomorrow. Folios she loved were pressed into single cards;
cards she doubted were fed to the stove. "Memory," she told her apprentice,
"is not what you keep. It is what you can find again before the question
finishes being asked."

国境の長いトンネルを抜けると雪国であった。夜の底が白くなった。信号所に汽車が止まった。
The wide characters above wrap differently — every glyph is two cells, and
the engine must break lines between them without orphaning half a character.

Words like Donaudampfschifffahrtsgesellschaftskapitänsmützenabzeichen exist
to be sliced: longer than any sane viewport, they must hard-wrap at grapheme
boundaries and still tween home when the column widens.

She kept a drawer of things that were almost true: a café receipt with no
café, a naïve résumé, coördinates for a city that moved. Combining marks and
diaereses ride along with their letters — zero cells wide, never orphaned.

When the stove was full and the room was light, the archivist would open one
card at random and let it unfold back into pages, just to watch the shelved
words slide aside to make room — the whole wall of text breathing in and out,
like something that remembered being alive.
